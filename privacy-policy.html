Privacy Policy

How Prime Ecommerce Hub collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: September 2026  |  Prime Ecommerce Hub, Sadiqabad, Pakistan

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information Prime Ecommerce Hub collects when you use our website, enroll in a course or contact us, how we use that information and how we protect it. By using our website or submitting any form, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

When you enroll in a course, apply for an internship or contact us, we collect the following information:

  • Full Name — required for enrollment and certificate issuance
  • Phone / WhatsApp Number — required for admission confirmation and student support
  • Email Address — required for communication and record keeping
  • CNIC / B-Form Number — required for certificate verification (enrollment only)
  • City — to understand where students are based
  • Course Selection — to process your enrollment in the correct program
  • Payment Method and Transaction Reference ID — to verify your fee payment (we do not store your bank credentials or card numbers)
  • Payment Screenshot — if submitted, this is referenced locally; it is not automatically uploaded to our servers
  • Internship Application Details — including education, area of interest and reason for applying
  • Messages and Inquiries — submitted through the contact form

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect only for the following purposes:

  • Processing and confirming your course or internship enrollment
  • Verifying fee payment and issuing enrollment confirmation
  • Issuing certificates of completion with verified identity details
  • Contacting you regarding your enrollment, class schedule or updates
  • Adding you to the relevant student WhatsApp support group
  • Responding to your inquiries submitted via the contact form
  • Maintaining administrative records in our secure dashboard

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Storage

Application and enrollment data submitted through our forms is stored in Google Firebase Firestore, a cloud database service provided by Google LLC. Firebase is subject to Google's own security and privacy standards. Student data is accessible only to authorized administrators through our password-protected admin dashboard.

Contact form submissions and messages are stored in the same Firebase Firestore database under restricted access.

5. Google Analytics

Our website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. Google Analytics collects anonymized data such as page views, session duration and device type. This data does not identify you personally. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

6. Cookies

Our website may use basic browser cookies or session storage to maintain your session state (for example, keeping you logged in to the admin dashboard within a single browser session). We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. The admin dashboard is password-protected. Firebase Firestore access is restricted through Firebase security rules. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

We retain enrollment and application data for as long as necessary to maintain student records and issue certificates. If you wish to request deletion of your personal data, please contact us at the email address below and we will address your request accordingly.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for students, graduates and professionals. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 years of age. If you believe a minor has submitted information through our website, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

10. Third-Party Links

Our website contains links to third-party platforms such as Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube and WhatsApp. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those platforms. Please review their respective privacy policies before sharing information with them.

11. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal or operational requirements)
  • Withdraw consent for communication at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us directly.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
