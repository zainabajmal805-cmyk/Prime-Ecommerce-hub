Who We Are

Prime Ecommerce Hub

Prime Ecommerce Hub is a dedicated e-commerce training and business solutions platform based in Sadiqabad, Pakistan. Founded with the mission to make digital business accessible to everyone, we provide practical, hands-on training that creates real results.

We specialize in marketplace selling, digital marketing, freelancing, web development and AI tools ? giving our students the exact skills needed to succeed in today's digital economy. Our training is not just theoretical ? every course is taught by practitioners with real selling experience on platforms like Amazon, Daraz, Shopify, eBay and Etsy.

Our Vision & Mission

Our blueprint for today and our roadmap for tomorrow

Vision

Prime Ecommerce Hub aims to become Pakistan's most trusted e-commerce training and business solutions platform -- producing thousands of successful online sellers, freelancers and digital entrepreneurs who build profitable businesses and contribute to the country's digital economy growth.

  • Pakistan's #1 E-Commerce Hub
  • Empower every digital entrepreneur
  • Build global selling skills locally

Mission

Prime Ecommerce Hub empowers every student, entrepreneur and business owner in Pakistan with practical e-commerce skills, expert mentorship and real-world support -- enabling them to build a sustainable online income through Amazon, Daraz, Shopify, eBay, freelancing and digital marketing.
